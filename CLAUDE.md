## Social posts
`src/lib/social.ts` (data + captions), `src/app/api/card/social` (1080x1350 cards; links must be signed via `cardUrl`), `src/lib/socialPush.ts` (runs from the news cron at 05:00 and 15:00 UTC). Two kinds only, to avoid spam (~170 slideshows a season):
- **Daily waiver wire** (15:00 UTC run): one carousel, each player shown once: cover ("Today's waiver wire", led by last night's best pickup; the brand name isn't the hook while it's new), more waiver heroes (<50% rostered, finished games only), trending adds/drops, minutes watch (minutes vs last season), injury pickups (real OUTs with a pickup, plus players back), and a call to action.
- **Breaking** (max one a day): a 50%+ rostered player ruled out with a <30% rostered pickup, or a 50%+ rostered player moved or cut.
No posts about role changes on stars or "still out" follow-ups (Wilson: obvious, priced-in news is not insightful). Branding says "your fantasy team", not ESPN; "% rostered across ESPN leagues" is fine as the data source. The slideshow goes to Instagram only; Threads and X get three standalone posts instead ("Drop your roster below" an hour after the adds post; replies to all three are answered by `src/lib/replyBot.ts` via Postroom's reply hook `/api/reply`), "Who ESPN managers are adding right now" and "Last night's waiver heroes" two hours later (on Threads, single-topic posts got 400-700 views, the slideshow ~40). Posts go to Postroom (Instagram, Threads, X), the owner's Telegram (/posts), Telegram users with news on (/nonews), and a daily email digest. Box-score minutes are saved daily (`social:minutes:<date>`) for a future minutes-trends slide.

## Player values
`src/lib/value.ts` is the model (Wilson's rules): chance he plays × minutes × points per minute. Windows (streams) count only current injury status; rest of season adds durability. Minutes follow recent box-score minutes (`social:minutes:<date>`) with temporary-role flags when a same-position regular is out with a return date. Rebounds/assists/stocks trust this season fast; scoring leans on the projection longer and sets the floor/ceiling. No penalty for bad teams. Durability comes from game logs (`src/lib/healthHistory.ts`, KV `value:health`, last 3 seasons, rotation seasons only): long absences (10+ games) are one-off injuries and don't count against him; many short absences mark him "in-and-out". Teammate dependence (`dependsOn`): for each injured regular on his team with a return date, the minutes he gains, measured from recent games with vs without the teammate and blended with #47's expected split (`shareMinutes`) while samples are thin; values before and after the return date use those minutes. The AI's news reads (`src/lib/autoTakes.ts` role multipliers on a take) adjust the starting expectation in `perGameCore`, then fade as games pile up; autoTakes bills ANTHROPIC_API_KEY (Claude credits) first, then OPENAI_API_KEY, then AI Gateway. Nightly fantasy points are saved as `social:fpts:<date>` for a future usage (points per minute) split. The per-game core (`src/lib/valueCore.ts`) is shared with the posts (`fppg`). The daily run saves predictions (`value:pred:<date>`) and scores them against the box scores (`value:err:<date>`). Used by the reply bot and /move; the in-season moves engine (`src/lib/inseason/streaming.ts`) still has its own `expectedFppg`.

## Breaking alerts
`src/lib/breaking.ts`, cron `/api/cron/breaking` every 10 minutes (`?dry=1` previews). Diffs ESPN's injury report (fetched fresh) against `breaking:status`; a 40%+ rostered player newly OUT for 3+ days triggers a Telegram alert (every linked user with news on, plus owner chats) and, for 70%+ rostered players, an email (max one per person per day). Pickups: teammates gaining 2+ minutes in the shared split (`shareMinutes`), ranked by value while he's out, under 60% rostered. Paying users with a connected league also see whether the pickup is free in their league (with the ESPN add link) and whether the injured player is theirs. Two sources: the report, and ESPN player news read by Claude (`src/lib/ai.ts` chain: ANTHROPIC_API_KEY first) for 40%+ rostered players whose `lastNewsDate` moved (one light players feed per check, at most 15 news reads), flagged only for extended absences (more than two games). Claude then picks the best pickup from the teammates the minutes split flags, with a one-line reason. One alert per player until he's healthy again (`breaking:sent:<id>`, cleared when he leaves the report). When the injured player is on a paying user's roster, the alert adds the best free agents in their league for the open IR spot (`src/lib/backupPlan.ts`: ranked by points over today through next matchup week). `/backup` on Telegram sends the standing shortlist by position. All-core rosters get no daily moves message (the engine never drops core players, and no moves means no message).
